## Brand Identity

### Brand Personality
**Professional Medical Technology**
- Clean, modern, and sophisticated
- Medical-grade precision and reliability
- Professional healthcare context
- Trustworthy and authoritative

### Core Values
- **Clarity**: Information should be immediately understandable
- **Precision**: Medical data requires accurate presentation
- **Professionalism**: Healthcare-grade visual standards
- **Accessibility**: Critical information must be easily accessible

### Target Audience
- Medical professionals (doctors, nurses, specialists)
- Healthcare administrators
- Medical students and researchers
